2026 Focus Areas

This Year's Key Themes

01 — Investing from Rwanda: Why and How?

The legal, financial, tax and operational conditions that make Rwanda a regional platform for investment and business development.

02 — Blending Public and Private Capital to Reduce SME Risk

Mechanisms combining public and private financing with guarantees to improve access to finance.

03 — AfCFTA Corridors: Financing Cross-Border Trade

Reducing barriers in cross-border payments, foreign exchange, transport and credit insurance across regional corridors.

04 — From Kigali to Brussels and Canada

The regulatory, commercial and quality requirements for successfully entering European, Canadian and other international markets.

05 — Youth and Fintech: Accelerating Financial Inclusion

Digital solutions that reduce costs, improve access to finance and support young businesses.

06 — Securing Cross-Border Transactions

From letter of intent to enforceable contract: the legal and compliance tools SMEs need to secure international commitments.
